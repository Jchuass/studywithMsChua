⚠️ Important Disclaimer
All questions in this app are AI-generated practice material. They are crafted by Claude AI (Anthropic) to align with the Singapore MOE syllabus structure, but they are:

✅ Aligned to MOE syllabus topics and question formats
✅ Useful for independent practice and revision
✅ Reviewed for accuracy before being included

❌ Not official MOE, SEAB or school examination questions
❌ Not endorsed by MOE, SEAB or any Singapore school
❌ Not a substitute for official past papers or teacher guidance

Always verify your answers with your teacher.
🏛️ What SEAB Publishes Freely
SEAB makes specimen papers free when a syllabus is revised — but actual past year papers must be purchased from authorised distributors.
Official · Free
SEAB Specimen Papers
Published free at seab.gov.sg when a syllabus is revised (available for 3 years). Currently includes O-Level E-Maths (4052), A-Maths (4049), and PSLE English oral specimen. These are the only official exam questions freely and legally available online.
Official · Free
SEAB Syllabus Documents
Full examination syllabuses for PSLE, O-Level and A-Level are published free at seab.gov.sg — listing every topic, question format, and mark allocation. Our AI uses these to structure every question.
Official · Paid
SEAB Past Year Papers
Actual past year PSLE and GCE papers are sold through authorised distributors listed on seab.gov.sg. The "Ten Year Series" (TYS) sold in Popular/MPH bookshops is the licensed version — the gold standard for exam prep.
🤖 How Our Questions Are Made
AI
Claude AI by Anthropic
Questions are generated by Claude Sonnet, instructed to follow SEAB syllabus topics, MOE question formats, and Singapore-specific contexts. The AI generates step-by-step solutions including Singapore Math bar model drawings for relevant topics.
Reference
SEAB Syllabus Documents (moe.gov.sg / seab.gov.sg)
Every batch of generated questions specifies the exact SEAB topic, level, question type and mark allocation — ensuring alignment with what is actually tested at each level.
Reference
Singapore Primary Mathematics Model Method
Bar models, number lines and ratio bars in our solutions follow the MOE Primary Mathematics model method framework — the same approach taught in Singapore primary schools.

Since questions are AI-generated, occasional errors in working or answers may occur. If you spot one:

1. Note the question topic and level
2. Check against your textbook or ask your teacher
3. Use the correct version for your revision
